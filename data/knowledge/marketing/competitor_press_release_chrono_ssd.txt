FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
ChronoTech Unveils ChronoSSD X-Series — A New Era of Storage Performance

San Jose, CA — March 18, 2026 — ChronoTech today announced the ChronoSSD
X-Series, a line of high-performance NVMe solid-state drives engineered for
creative professionals and demanding workloads. ChronoSSD X is available
immediately in 1TB, 2TB, and 4TB capacities at retail and through
ChronoTech's authorized partners.

"With ChronoSSD X, we are redefining what storage means for the modern
creator," said Jordan Reyes, CEO of ChronoTech. "Our customers told us they
were tired of waiting on their tools. ChronoSSD X eliminates that wait."

KEY MESSAGING THEMES:
- "Creator-First" — every feature framed around the working creative
- "Eliminate the wait" — speed positioned as time, not megabytes
- "Reliability you can publish on" — trust and warranty as differentiators
- "Built in California" — heritage and quality manufacturing emphasis

PRODUCT HIGHLIGHTS (per ChronoTech's claims):
- Sequential read speeds up to 7,200 MB/s
- Sequential write speeds up to 6,800 MB/s
- PCIe Gen 5 NVMe 2.0 interface
- 5-year warranty with on-site replacement for studio customers
- Proprietary "ChronoCool" thermal design

PRICING (MSRP):
- ChronoSSD X 1TB: $189
- ChronoSSD X 2TB: $339
- ChronoSSD X 4TB: $599

POSITIONING NOTES (from public messaging):
ChronoTech is positioning ChronoSSD X squarely against high-end NVMe drives
from established storage brands. Their go-to-market emphasizes the on-site
replacement program and a "creator community" partner network. They have
not announced an 8TB SKU and have not published independent third-party
benchmark results.

About ChronoTech:
Founded in 2018, ChronoTech designs and manufactures storage solutions for
creative professionals. The company is headquartered in San Jose, California.
