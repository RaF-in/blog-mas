Product Specification Sheet: Project QuantumDrive

This document is the single source of truth for product facts. The Writer
must never invent numbers; if a number is not in this sheet, it does not
exist. Last verified: 2026-04-01.

Product Name: QuantumDrive Q-1
Product Family: QuantumDrive
Form Factor: M.2 2280
Storage Capacity: 2TB, 4TB, 8TB
Sequential Read Speed: up to 7,500 MB/s
Sequential Write Speed: up to 7,000 MB/s
Random Read (4K): up to 1,200,000 IOPS
Random Write (4K): up to 1,100,000 IOPS
Interface: NVMe 2.0, PCIe Gen 5 x4
Endurance (TBW): 1,500 (2TB) / 3,000 (4TB) / 6,000 (8TB)
MTBF: 2,000,000 hours
Warranty: 5 years limited
Operating Temperature: 0°C to 70°C
Power Consumption: 7.5W typical, 9.5W peak
Encryption: AES-256 hardware encryption, TCG Opal 2.0
Controller: Phison E26 with custom QuantumDrive firmware
DRAM Cache: 4GB LPDDR4 (8TB variant)
SLC Cache: Dynamic, up to 25% of free capacity

Pricing (MSRP, USD):
- QD-Q1-2TB: $249
- QD-Q1-4TB: $449
- QD-Q1-8TB: $799

Target Customers:
- Professional video editors working in 8K / RAW workflows
- 3D artists running real-time rendering and asset streaming
- Photographers handling 100MP+ RAW catalogues
- Game developers compiling large engines and asset bundles

Compatibility:
- Windows 11 23H2 and later
- macOS 14.0 and later (via Thunderbolt 4 enclosure for Apple Silicon)
- Linux kernel 6.5+

Notes:
- The 2TB variant has lower TBW endurance and is positioned for prosumers,
  not 24/7 professional rigs. Marketing copy targeting studios should
  default to the 4TB or 8TB SKUs.
- Sequential read speeds require a PCIe Gen 5 motherboard. On Gen 4 systems
  the drive operates at up to 7,000 MB/s read. State this clearly in
  technical copy; omit only in lifestyle / aspirational copy.
