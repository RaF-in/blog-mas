Social Media Campaign Brief: QuantumDrive Q-1 Launch — Q1 2026

Campaign Name: "Finish Faster"
Launch Window: 2026-04-15 through 2026-06-30
Owner: marketing@company.example
Status: Approved

OBJECTIVE
Drive 50,000 product detail page visits and 4,000 pre-orders during the
launch window. Build a category narrative that QuantumDrive is the
default storage choice for serious creative professionals.

PRIMARY AUDIENCE
- Professional video editors (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro power users)
- 3D artists and motion designers (Cinema 4D, Blender, Houdini)
- Photographers shooting 100MP+ medium format
- Studio IT decision-makers buying drives in volume

SECONDARY AUDIENCE
- Prosumer creators (YouTube production, freelancers)
- PC enthusiast / hardware reviewer community

KEY MESSAGES (in priority order)
1. Time is the real bottleneck. QuantumDrive gives you that time back.
2. Studio-grade endurance, not consumer-grade compromise.
3. PCIe Gen 5 performance with a 5-year warranty.

CHANNELS AND CADENCE
- LinkedIn: 3 posts/week, decision-maker tone, case studies
- X/Twitter: 5 posts/week, short benchmarks, creator quotes
- YouTube: 6 short-form videos, real workflow demos
- Reddit (r/editors, r/3Dmodeling): organic engagement only, no ad copy

TONE
Confident and aspirational, per the brand style guide. Lead with what the
creator can now *do*, not with the spec sheet. Specs are the supporting
evidence, never the headline.

DO NOT
- Compare directly to ChronoSSD X by name in headlines
- Use the word "guaranteed" without legal review
- Post benchmarks without a link to the methodology

SUCCESS CRITERIA
- Pre-orders: 4,000 by 2026-06-30
- Earned media mentions: 25 from tier-1 creator publications
- Brand-tracking lift on "fastest creative storage": +15 points
